First stable release.

- The Cyberspace Jukebox as a music app: latest tracks, most saved, search, genres and artists
- Background playback with notification, lock screen and headset controls
- Now Playing with lyrics, an Up next you can reorder, and autoplay of similar songs
- Optional sign-in for members-only shared tracks
- When YouTube limits your network: switches to IPv4 by itself and resumes when it can
- Eight Cyberspace themes
