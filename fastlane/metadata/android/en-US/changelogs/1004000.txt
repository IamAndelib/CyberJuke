- Local music: play songs from folders you choose on your phone, as songs, albums and artists, offline too
- Playlists: make your own from any songs; play, reorder, rename them in Library
- Library is a list of Liked, Recently played, Local and your playlists, or a grid of tiles
- Liked and Local sort Newest or A–Z
- The queue shows Shuffled and Repeat beside its title
- Artists with a comma in their name (Earth, Wind & Fire) stay one artist
- Many smaller fixes
