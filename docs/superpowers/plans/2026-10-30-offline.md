# Offline 1.3.6 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** With liked songs saved, the app plays, skips, shows art and says true things offline, and liked songs stay saved however much else is streamed.

**Architecture:**
- **Native owns playback offline.** One pure skip helper is used on every forward path. A second, never-evicting SimpleCache (`saved/`) holds liked songs, read before the LRU listening cache. Covers of saved songs are plain files, served to the notification by a `BitmapLoader` and to the WebView by a `BridgeWebViewClient`.
- **The web guard and screens follow native's saved set,** with passive, offline-aware copy.

**Tech Stack:**
- Web: Preact 11 + @preact/signals, Vitest, Playwright.
- Android: Kotlin, Media3 1.11.1 (ExoPlayer, SimpleCache, MediaSession), Capacitor 8.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-offline-design.md` (approved 30 Oct 2026, all defaults).

## Review Focus

1. **Offline with only unsaved songs ahead:** Next is refused with the existing toast, and native never loops. Pinned by `OfflineNextTest.allUnsavedGivesNull` and `offlineGuard.test` "no saved ahead refuses".
2. **Going online mid-skip:** online, the skip helper never runs and Next is plain ExoPlayer Next. Pinned by `OfflineNextTest`'s callers being gated on `!AudioCache.online`, and the e2e "online Next plays the unsaved song".
3. **Upgrading from 1.3.5 with liked songs already saved:** none of them stops playing offline, during the copy or after it. Pinned by Task 3.2's `SavePlanTest.migrationCopiesAnyQuality` and the Preview 2 phone check.
4. **An empty liked list after a failed load:** the saved store isn't wiped. Pinned by `SavedReconcileTest.emptyLikedKeepsAll`.
5. **A cover URL outside the allowlist in `covers[]`:** it's ignored, never fetched or served. Pinned by `ArtStoreTest.rejectsForeignHosts`.
