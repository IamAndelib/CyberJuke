# Testing a preview on your phone

Install **CyberJuke Preview** from <https://github.com/IamAndelib/CyberJuke/releases/tag/preview> (see [PUBLISHING.md, step 5](PUBLISHING.md#5-test-the-preview)). Tick each box as you go. Anything that's wrong or feels off is worth reporting.

**How to report:** what you did → what you expected → what happened. Add a screenshot or screen recording, and say whether you were on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

## Install
- [ ] It installs next to the regular CyberJuke (if you have it) and shows as **CyberJuke Preview**, with its icon.
- [ ] The first start shows Home with tracks within a few seconds.
- [ ] Opening the app shows the jukebox on black (also on Android 12 and newer: not a plain or white icon), then Home, with no black or empty screen in between. With Settings → Theme **Light**, close the app completely and open it: after the jukebox, Home shows in the light theme at once, with no black flash. The jukebox never stays more than a couple of seconds, even on a slow connection.

## Playback
- [ ] A Home track plays. So do a genre track, an artist page track and a **Global** search result.
- [ ] A **Jukebox MP3** plays (Search "Träume" or "Anniversary Mix", both latex.gg MP3s): its square cover shows in the list, in Now Playing, the notification and on the lock screen; seeking works; it moves on to the next track at its end. Swipe the app away and reopen it: it comes back, paused, where it was.
- [ ] With the screen off and the app in the background, music keeps playing and moves on to the next track.
- [ ] Notification and lock screen: play/pause, next and previous work, the seek bar moves and seeks, and they show the title and artwork. Check once on Android 13 or newer and once on Android 12 or older.
- [ ] While music plays, the status bar shows the CyberJuke jukebox (a small arched box), not a music note; so does the notification's header.
- [ ] On a fresh install (Android 13 or newer), the first play asks for no permission, and the notification still shows its controls.
- [ ] The notification (expanded) and the lock screen show **Shuffle** and **Repeat** beside the usual buttons. Tap Shuffle: it lights up, and Now Playing's shuffle is on too. Tap Repeat three times: all, one, off, the icon changing each time, and Now Playing follows. Change them in the app: the notification follows.
- [ ] Unplugging headphones or disconnecting Bluetooth pauses playback.
- [ ] Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data **during** a song: playback continues, or resumes by itself within a few seconds. No "YouTube is limiting requests" banner.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode during a song until it stops, then off again: it carries on by itself.
- [ ] With Bluetooth or wired headphones, turn on airplane mode during a song until **Waiting for carrier…** shows, then press the headphones' play/pause button once: the wait stops (the app shows Play), and airplane mode off doesn't start the music. On Android 12 or older, the notification's Play button still tries to play.
- [ ] Start a Jukebox track (and once a genre's Play), swipe the app away, and press Next on the notification 30 times or so: it keeps going (about 50 songs are lined up as the app leaves the screen). Reopen the app near the end of them: Up next fills up again.
- [ ] Settings → Audio quality shows **Auto** (on an updated install that had High, too; Low stays Low). **Auto**, **High** and **Low** each play, and the line under the name says what the choice does.
- [ ] With **Auto** on a slow connection (mobile data with weak signal, or a throttled Wi-Fi): once playback has stalled twice within a few minutes, the next tracks sound lighter, one step at a time, never mid-track. Back on a fast connection, it climbs back over several tracks (5 calm minutes per step). On a connection that can't keep the higher quality, the stalls after each try come further and further apart (at most one every 40 minutes or so).
- [ ] Settings → **Crossfade** is **Off** on a fresh or updated install: tracks follow each other as before.
- [ ] Settings → Crossfade at **3 s**, then **6 s**, then **10 s**, on **Jukebox shuffle**: listen across 3 transitions with each. The old song fades all the way down while the next rises from silence, smoothly (no steps in the volume you can hear), with no gap and no dip or bump in the overall loudness in the middle. Neither track ever pauses by itself during a fade. Do it once on the phone's speaker and once on Bluetooth headphones.
- [ ] With Crossfade on, the old song stays on screen through the fade, and switches to the new one as the old one ends (the new one then shows a few seconds in): in Now Playing, the mini player, the notification and on the lock screen. Up next moves on with it.
- [ ] With Crossfade on, during a fade: **pause**, then play again: both tracks stop together and go on together. **Next**: the song fading in carries on from where it is, without starting again, and rises to full volume while the old one fades out quickly (on **Repeat one**, the next song starts from its beginning). Another list: its song starts from its beginning at full volume. **Previous** or a **seek** (in Now Playing): the song fading in is gone at once and the old one plays from there at full volume. Tapping **next** outside a fade changes tracks at once, as before.
- [ ] With Crossfade on, have someone call you during a fade (or set an alarm to go off then): both tracks stop for the call, and the fade finishes afterwards. Tap **Add to queue** during that call: afterwards the music goes on by itself (the old song, at full volume; the fade is skipped). A navigation prompt during a fade: both tracks dip under it (on Android 7 only the old one does, expected).
- [ ] With Crossfade on, turn on airplane mode during a fade: the old track plays out its end, and the new one plays what it has (or shows **Waiting for carrier…** and carries on once you're back).
- [ ] With Crossfade on, **Add to queue** during a fade: the song fading in becomes the current one at once (the title switches a little early; the old song fades out within a quarter of a second, no click), and the queued song plays after it. With **Shuffle** on, and while autoplay adds tracks, the fade goes on undisturbed. Remove the song that is fading in: it stops at once and the old one plays on at full volume; do it once in the fade's last second and listen for a gap at the old song's end (report one).
- [ ] Crossfade at **10 s**: drag the seekbar to about 6 s before a song's end: it fades into the next song over those last seconds, ending with the song (the log says `Crossfade started: 5900 ms` or so). Drag it into the last two seconds: no fade, the next song starts as usual. Drag it to about 3 s before the end: a short fade, with no click or gap in the old song.
- [ ] With Crossfade on and **Repeat one**: the end of the track fades into its own start. The last track of a list (Autoplay off, Repeat off) plays to its end, without a fade.
- [ ] Crossfade at **10 s**, a list where the next track can't play (a removed or private video; old Jukebox posts sometimes are): the current track plays to its very end, without a fade (the log says `Crossfade skipped: NEXT_NOT_READY`), and the dead track is skipped as with Crossfade Off.
- [ ] With Crossfade on, swipe the app away from recent apps during a fade, then reopen it: the music goes on, and the app opens on the new track.
- [ ] With Crossfade on and the phone on a computer (USB debugging): `adb logcat -s CyberJukeCrossfade:W` prints `Crossfade started: N ms` for each fade (6000 at 6 s, less for a short track), and `Crossfade skipped: REASON` for each track end that didn't fade (`LAST`, `SAME_ALBUM`, `OUTAGE` or `NEXT_NOT_READY`, say). Report any skip you didn't expect.
- [ ] Settings → **Gapless playback** is **on** on a fresh or updated install. Play a Global album (Crossfade Off) and listen across 3 transitions: each track follows the last without a pause (a debug build logs `Transition seamless`). On mobile data, the next track's data is fetched while the current one plays, as before.
- [ ] Gapless playback **off**, Crossfade Off: each track ends, then after a short pause (the next one buffering its first seconds, a few seconds plus the round trip; no spinner that lasts) the next one plays; a debug build logs `Transition stalled: N ms`. Skip through a few tracks while watching the phone's data use (Settings → Network → App data usage): the next track costs little (its first chunk, under a megabyte) until it starts. Airplane mode at a track's end: **Waiting for carrier…** shows, and it carries on once you're back. Also with a track of 8 minutes or more: the next track's connection is held open idle until the end, and if the server closed it meanwhile the pause is longer (a retry, then a fresh link) but the next track still plays.
- [ ] Gapless playback **off**, Crossfade at **6 s**: the tracks fade into each other as with Gapless on (Crossfade readies the next track early); no pause.
- [ ] Gapless playback **on**, Crossfade at **6 s**, play a Global **album**: its tracks go straight into each other, no fade. The last track of the album fades into what autoplay plays next; a Global playlist or a Jukebox list fades between every pair.
- [ ] Gapless playback **off**, Crossfade at **6 s**, the same album: it fades between the album's tracks.
- [ ] Gapless playback **on**, Crossfade at **6 s**, mid-album: swipe the app away from recent apps and reopen it. The restored album's tracks still go straight into each other, as before the restart.

What "gapless" does not do: the app inserts nothing between two tracks, but Media3 leaves the codec's own padding, some 10 to 50 ms, on YouTube's streams (its fMP4 reader turns no edit list into gapless info; Opus's tail padding is only trimmed in offload). A very tight musical join may still be heard as a tiny bump; no player built on these does better. Media3's own preloading of the next item (`setPreloadConfiguration`) isn't used: the app already buffers the next track ahead.
- [ ] Settings → **IPv4**: Always, then Off, then back to **Auto**: tracks play each time.
- [ ] On a phone or network that showed "YouTube is limiting requests" before: play for a while with IPv4 on **Auto**. The banner should rarely show; if it does, playback **resumes by itself** when a short countdown (1 or 3 min) ends, also with the screen off (keep the app in the background to check). Unplug your headphones during the countdown: it then stays paused.
- [ ] If the banner shows: tap **[Try now]**. It goes away; music resumes, or the banner comes back with a longer wait. Switching Wi-Fi ↔ mobile data also lifts it at once.
- [ ] Settings shows a **Connection** line under IPv4 (IPv4 or IPv6, and the last limit, if any).
- [ ] Pause a track right after starting it: it stays paused once it has loaded.
- [ ] Fling a long list and tap the search button (or a tab) while it is still moving: it opens on the first tap.

## Queue and autoplay
- [ ] Tap a track on Home. **Up next** shows similar songs under "Autoplay · similar to …" (same artist or genre feel).
- [ ] Play an album from an artist page. It plays in order, with no Autoplay section until its last few tracks; then autoplay continues after the last track.
- [ ] **Add to queue** two tracks, then start a different list. Your two queued tracks still play next.
- [ ] In Up next, **Remove** a track, then tap **Undo**. It comes back in the same place.
- [ ] Play an album and skip three songs. Now Playing's Queue shows **[+] Played · 3** above the playing song; tap it to open. Move a played song with ↑ ↓, remove one and tap **Undo** (it comes back in the same place), then ↓ on the last played song: it plays next. With **Repeat all**, the next lap plays the played songs in their new order. With **Shuffle** on, played songs show only ✕.
- [ ] On Home, pick a genre chip: the Shuffle box reads "Shuffle <genre>". Tap it: only that genre plays, and past the first 50 more of it follow (Autoplay on). With **All**: "Shuffle the Jukebox", from everything.
- [ ] Turn on **Repeat all**, then tap a song on Home (and a genre with a single song): Up next fills with its suggestions and says "Repeating the queue." under its heading; after the last one the song plays again. No more are added meanwhile.
- [ ] Play a genre with **Repeat all** on: after its last song the first plays again, with no suggestions in between. Turn repeat off near the end: suggestions appear.
- [ ] **Repeat one**: the song replays when it ends and Up next says "Repeating this song."; Next moves on. With Autoplay off, at a list's last song Next is greyed out in the app and on the notification alike.
- [ ] Settings → **Autoplay** off: playback stops when the list ends. A tap on a Home song plays Home's list on from it; a genre with one song says "Autoplay is off." in Up next.
- [ ] In Up next, tap the 4th suggestion: it plays, and the three it jumped over come next, then the 5th. Remove a few suggestions: they don't come back later.
- [ ] Tap a song on Home and wait for its suggestions, then turn on **Shuffle**: the heading reads **Queue · Shuffled** and the suggestions are mixed; turn it off: they're back in their order. Start an album at track 3, tap a suggestion, then turn Shuffle on: the album's tracks still to come stay in Up next, mixed, before the suggestions; nothing you heard comes back.
- [ ] Press and hold a song in Now Playing's queue (Played, the playing one, Up next): the same menu as on Home opens.
- [ ] Offline in Liked: only saved songs follow, and Up next dims any that can't play.
- [ ] Let autoplay run for an hour or more: it keeps going without repeating the same songs.

## Signal Radio
- [ ] The **Radio** tab comes after Library; all five tab labels show whole (also on a small phone). Settings opens from the icon at the top right of Home (no label), with no tab highlighted while it shows, and Back returns to Home. From Settings, tap Genres, then Home: Home shows, not Settings. The Radio tab's header says "by hyphen8d", with nothing under the station list. Airplane mode on: the stations dim (the one playing stays bright), and tapping one says "No carrier. Connect to play." It lists the YM and ZM bands, each station with its frequency, name and tagline.
- [ ] Tap a station: it plays one of its tracks, the station is marked with a radio icon, and Now Playing says "Playing from Signal Radio · " and its name. Up next shows the rest of the station, then "More from …".
- [ ] Tap the station that's on: Now Playing opens, and the music doesn't restart; paused, the tap plays it on. Tap another station: its tracks play, and the mark moves.
- [ ] On the Radio tab, Search (Here) finds a song from any station; tapping it plays that song.
- [ ] The ⓘ button shows what the station plays and how many tracks it has; **Tune in** starts it.
- [ ] Let a station play with the screen off for longer than its round (about 2 hours for a 32-track station): it keeps going, a new shuffle of the same station.
- [ ] Swipe the app away while a station plays and reopen it: Now Playing and Up next show the station's titles, not "Unknown track".
- [ ] Settings → **Autoplay** off, then tune in and let it play past its first round: it keeps going. A Jukebox list still stops at its end.

## Browsing and gestures
- [ ] Open a genre page and tap ★ in the top bar: the genre shows under ★ Favourites on the Genres tab. Same on an artist page.
- [ ] Now Playing: the ♥ sits beside the title and artist; a long title scrolls without running under it.
- [ ] Open the ⋯ menu in Now Playing and drag on the menu and on the dark area above it: nothing behind it scrolls.
- [ ] Search for a poster's @name: nothing. Search for an artist or a song: found.
- [ ] Settings → Licenses → Full license texts opens and scrolls.
- [ ] Pull down on Home to refresh: smooth, with no jumping.
- [ ] Tap an artist or a genre (from Home, Search or Now Playing): it opens on the tab you're on, and **Back** returns exactly where you were, at the same scroll position.
- [ ] Android back (gesture or button), step by step: it closes the ⋯ menu, then Now Playing, then the page you opened, then goes from another tab (or Settings) to Home. On Home with nothing open, swipe back slowly: on Android 15 and newer (13 and 14: with Developer options → Predictive back animations on) the app shrinks towards the home screen as you swipe; let go and the app goes to the background with the music still playing; reopen it: it's as you left it, not reloaded. Start the same swipe and pull it back: the app stays. On Android 12 or older, back on Home also sends the app to the background, without the animation, and it isn't reloaded when you come back.
- [ ] Quick taps show a press: Settings, Back, chips, rows, tiles, ♥, shuffle, repeat, switches, and **[Undo]** (inside the message's frame, not to its edge). Settings, Back, **[Clear all]**, a recent search's ✕ and Up next's arrows show it for a moment before the screen changes. The tab bar shows none. Starting to scroll a list doesn't flash its rows. Nothing vibrates.
- [ ] Tap the tab you're already on: it scrolls to the top. Tap again: it goes back to that tab's first page.
- [ ] Long-press a track row: its menu opens, and releasing your finger doesn't tap anything in the menu.
- [ ] Long titles scroll slowly in Now Playing and in the mini player.
- [ ] Long-pressing text (headers, titles) doesn't select it.
- [ ] Swipe up on the mini player: Now Playing opens. Swipe Now Playing down and let go: it carries on down and closes in one motion.
- [ ] In Now Playing, start a scroll (up or down) on the seek bar: the page scrolls and the thumb doesn't move. A sideways drag seeks; a tap seeks to that spot.
- [ ] Unlike a track, unfavourite (★) a genre or artist, and clear history: each one offers **Undo**. Clearing history and signing out ask you first.
- [ ] Liking a track or favouriting (★) a genre or artist shows a short "added" message with no Undo, and the star or heart fills at once, even with a long Artists list. On the Genres and Artists tabs the starred tile moves up into ★ Favourites straight away (not also left in the grid), and unstarring puts it back in its place.
- [ ] Swipe an **Undo** message at the bottom (e.g. after unliking) left, then another right: each one goes. A short drag springs back. A swipe that starts on **Undo** doesn't undo. Right after a swipe, one tap on ♥ or a ★ works (a fast flick too).
- [ ] Only one message shows at a time: star several genres quickly, and each new message replaces the last. Like a song in Now Playing, then scroll or tap anywhere else: the message goes at once. Its **Undo** still works.
- [ ] Settings → **Updates**: the automatic check is off on a fresh install and after upgrading from 1.1.x, and nothing asks GitHub until you turn it on or tap **Check now**; **Check now** says "Up to date (x.y.z)" or, when a newer release is out, shows it at the top of Settings with **Download**, and a dot on the Settings button at the top right of Home. Turning the toggle on stays on after a restart. Installed from F-Droid, the card only says updates come through F-Droid.
- [ ] The ⋯ menu of a liked song says **Unlike**; of any other song, **Like**. After **Next**, the Now Playing ♥ shows the new song's state.
- [ ] Every theme (Settings → Theme) is readable.
- [ ] Settings → Theme **Light**, then **Bubblegum**: the clock and icons at the top and the navigation buttons or bar at the bottom are dark and easy to see; on **Dark** and **C64** they are light. Turn the phone sideways and back, and switch the phone's dark mode on and off: they stay that way. Close the app completely and open it: the screen doesn't shift down after it shows, and there's no grey or white strip above or below the app.

## Account (if you use Cyberspace sign-in)
- [ ] Sign in: tracks marked **[members]** appear.
- [ ] Close the app completely and reopen it: you're still signed in.
- [ ] Sign out: the [members] tracks disappear everywhere, including Liked and the queue.
- [ ] Sign in again with the same account: the [members] tracks you had liked are back in Liked, where they were, and the ones you played are back in Recently played.
- [ ] With a second account: sign out, sign in as the other one (none of the first account's [members] likes or plays show), like and play something, sign out, and sign in as the first again: its own are back, the other's aren't.

## Rough conditions
- [ ] Airplane mode on Home: the top bar reads "[ offline ] no carrier · connect to play", and the rows stay, fading to dim; nothing disappears. Airplane mode off: they're bright again.
- [ ] Close the app, turn on airplane mode, open it: Home, the Genres and Artists tabs and Search show **[ NO CARRIER ]** (on Genres and Artists in the middle of the screen, with no intro line). Airplane mode off: the lists load by themselves.
- [ ] With the app open, switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data (turn Wi-Fi off, then on): no offline bar, and no list disappears or dims.
- [ ] Airplane mode, then tap a track in Library (its rows are dimmed), Play, or the Jukebox shuffle: "No carrier. Connect to play.", and nothing starts. The mini player's Play does the same; Pause still works.
- [ ] Force-stop the app, turn on Airplane mode and open it with nothing playing. A track's ⋯ → **Add to queue**: a saved liked song plays; any other says to connect, and nothing starts.
- [ ] Play a track, then turn on airplane mode until it stops: the message starts with "Signal lost" (not "Can't play"). Airplane mode off: it carries on by itself.
- [ ] **Liked songs offline.** On Wi-Fi, like a song and let it play to the end (or a minute after liking it). Airplane mode on: the top bar reads "[ offline ] no carrier · your liked songs still play"; tapping it opens Library → Liked, where that song isn't dimmed and plays (the queue holds only saved liked songs). In Recently played it is bright too and plays from there. A dimmed one says "Not saved yet. Needs a connection."; Home's Shuffle says "No carrier. Your liked songs still play." and its **Liked** button opens Liked. Pause a song that isn't saved, turn Airplane mode on and press Play: it resumes from what it loaded.
- [ ] **A liked Jukebox MP3 offline.** On Wi-Fi, like "Träume" (a latex.gg MP3) and let it play to the end (or wait a minute after liking it). Airplane mode on: in Library → Liked it is bright and plays, from the phone, seeking included. A liked MP3 that hasn't been saved yet is dimmed and says "Not saved yet. Needs a connection." If a members-only post is an MP3 you liked and saved, signing out removes its saved audio: offline it is dimmed too.
- [ ] **Liked songs wait for Wi-Fi.** Settings → **Download using mobile data** is **off** on a fresh or updated install. On mobile data, like a few songs (a Jukebox MP3 mix among them) without playing them: Android's mobile data usage for CyberJuke barely moves, and offline they are dimmed. On Wi-Fi, with the app open, they are saved a little later (Airplane mode: bright in Liked). Turn the setting **on** and like another song on mobile data: it is saved within a minute (Airplane mode: bright). Turn it off while a long one is saving on mobile data: the download stops within a second or so (Android's data usage for CyberJuke stops growing), and it goes on from there on Wi-Fi. On Wi-Fi with an always-on VPN (WireGuard, Orbot), like a song: note whether it is saved (Airplane mode: bright). A VPN Android marks as metered counts as mobile data.
- [ ] **A long liked mix.** With Listening cache at 128 MB, a mix bigger than 96 MB (well over an hour) isn't saved at all: it stays dimmed offline, and the liked songs saved before stay bright.
- [ ] **A long track doesn't push out liked songs.** Settings → Listening cache 128 MB, with liked songs saved (Airplane mode: bright in Liked). Back online, play a long Jukebox mix or video you haven't liked (an hour or more) for a few minutes, then a few short tracks. Airplane mode on: the liked songs saved before are all still bright in Liked.
- [ ] Mid-song, airplane mode on: the current track plays to its end and the next one plays too (both are in the listening cache), then it waits for carrier.
- [ ] Settings → Listening cache → **Clear cache** asks first (Cancel keeps everything). Confirm it while a liked song plays, on Wi-Fi: it keeps playing, and shortly after it has loaded whole it is saved again (Airplane mode on: it alone of the liked songs is bright). On mobile data (Download using mobile data off) it isn't saved again until you're on Wi-Fi. Back on Wi-Fi with the app open for a while (and a few songs played): liked songs come back undimmed offline, one at a time, newest like first. On mobile data, older likes are not downloaded.
- [ ] Settings → Listening cache: 512, then 128 MB while playing: nothing stops, and liked songs saved before stay saved as long as they fit.
- [ ] **Low storage.** Fill the phone's storage until Android warns about it (copy a few large files over), then play tracks that haven't played before: they keep playing from the network, with no error and no skipped track (the cache just can't keep them). Free the space: tracks are kept again.
- [ ] **Saved songs Android can't clear.** On Wi-Fi, like 3 songs and let each play to the end (or wait a minute after liking it), so they're saved. Force-stop the app (Settings → Apps → CyberJuke Preview → Force stop), then make Android clear the apps' caches: from a computer, `adb shell pm trim-caches 999G`; without one, fill the storage until Android warns about it. Airplane mode on, open the app: in Library → Liked the 3 songs are bright and play.
- [ ] **The song the app reopens on.** Play a Home track you haven't liked, pause it, force-stop the app and turn on airplane mode. Open the app: the mini player shows that track, paused, and its row in Recently played is dimmed. Play (mini player, Now Playing or the row) says "No carrier…" and nothing tries to play (no "Signal lost", no Waiting for carrier). Do the same with a saved liked song: it is bright and plays.
- [ ] **Updating keeps saved songs.** With the previous Preview installed and some liked songs saved (bright in airplane mode), install this one over it. Airplane mode on, open: the same songs are bright and play, without downloading them again. Settings → Apps → CyberJuke Preview → Storage: the listening cache counts as the app's data (User data), not as Cache. On a debug build, `adb shell run-as io.github.iamandelib.cyberjuke ls -lnd no_backup/audio` shows the app's own group (the same number as its user) and `drwxrwx--x`.
- [ ] **Rough network.** Throttle the connection (a phone hotspot on 2G/3G, or a router's bandwidth limit) and play for 15 minutes: no track is skipped and playback doesn't stop; any stall is short and picks up by itself.
- [ ] Walk out of Wi-Fi range (or block the router's internet while Wi-Fi stays connected) mid-song: the song keeps playing for a minute or two from what's buffered. If it does stop, Now Playing and the mini player show **Waiting for carrier…** in place of the time and the artist, and it plays on by itself once you're back in range (it keeps trying for 9 minutes, also when Wi-Fi still says connected). Do it once with the screen locked: it still carries on by itself, without waiting for you to turn the screen on.
- [ ] Wi-Fi ↔ mobile data handover mid-song, a few times in a row: playback continues or resumes by itself; the next track starts without a long wait. **Waiting for carrier…** doesn't flash during the switch (it shows only once a stall has lasted a second).
- [ ] **Wi-Fi with a login page** (a café, a hotel): join it with the app open, before logging in. The lists you open say **[ NO CARRIER ]** and the music, once its buffer runs out, says "Signal lost" and shows **Waiting for carrier…**, with no offline bar (the phone says it's connected). Log in on the portal page and come back to the app: the list loads by itself and the music carries on, with no tap on Redial.
- [ ] Switch Wi-Fi off and on while a genre page is loading (a request caught in the switch): the page still fills in by itself, with no error and no Redial.
- [ ] Settings → Audio quality **Low**, like a song and let it save (play it to the end on Wi-Fi), then switch to **Auto**, close the app completely, reopen it in airplane mode and play the song from Liked: it plays, from the phone, with no download.
- [ ] Like a song on Wi-Fi and turn airplane mode on while it is still saving (a few seconds in). Back online, with the app open, it is saved a little later by itself (airplane mode on again: it's bright in Liked), with no message in between.
- [ ] While **Waiting for carrier…** shows, the button shows Pause (also offline): press it and the text goes, and the music stays paused when the connection comes back. With the phone still connected (Wi-Fi without internet), press **Play** after that: it tries at once; once music plays the text is gone (if the connection is still down, the wait comes back). Unplug your headphones while it waits: it stays paused when the connection comes back.
- [ ] Leave it waiting with no connection: after about 9 minutes the text goes and it no longer starts by itself; **Play** still works once you're back.
- [ ] With Crossfade on, a track's end while **Waiting for carrier…**: no fade (the log says `Crossfade skipped: OUTAGE`); the next track starts the usual way once it can. On a throttled connection where the next track takes a while to start: the old track plays out its end, and the new one fades in once it plays (if the next track isn't ready at all when the fade is due, no fade: `NEXT_NOT_READY`).
- [ ] After an hour of playback on the Preview (once with Crossfade Off, once at 6 s), Android's battery and memory use for CyberJuke (Settings → Apps → CyberJuke Preview) is about what the current release shows.
- [ ] While music plays, swipe the app away from recent apps, then reopen it: Now Playing and Up next show the right tracks (no "Unknown track").
- [ ] Skip quickly through 10 tracks: no "YouTube is limiting requests" banner.
- [ ] If that banner ever appears, write down the time and your network. It's YouTube limiting your connection, and the app waits instead of hammering it.
